Torb District

The idea was simple — to turn an ordinary street in the city center into a space where graffiti becomes part of the environment and brings the walls to life.

It started with a single piece; over time, the area turned into an open-air gallery. There’s no fixed template — each work adds a new layer and a new story.

The main characters are ducks — locals call it “Duck Stories.” The walls change every season: new letters, new faces, new energy.
